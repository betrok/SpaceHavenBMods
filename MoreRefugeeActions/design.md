# More refugee actions

There is not much you can do with refugees in game, especially in friendly manner.

Your options are limited to:
* Return to theirs faction ship or station for reward. Good option but it requires finding location where that can be done while each can accept only few at time. For minor/story factions there is straight up not enough places in galaxy to take them all sometimes.
* Sell to slavers. More bitter way but gameplay-wise very similar to previous option.
* Imprison refugee into all the normal prisoner interactions.
* Kill or leave to die. Easy way about it but we are good guys, right?.. And apparently leaving refugees on theirs own on fully stocked hyperspace ready ship means that they will die too.

In meanwhile they will eat resources of your ship and at best cosplay logistic bots as long as living conditions are good enough. And if conditions are not particularly good, which trends to happen when ship is suddenly overloaded with more persons than it was designed to support, mental breakdowns can lead to destructive behavior.

## Proposed additional alternatives

### Drop off on any non-hostile leisure or trade station

Expands peaceful options to part with refuge. Surely they figure it out on theirs own form there.

Half of normal return reward, only relationship points and credits. Can not be done if you still have refugees of faction that owns station. Maybe smaller limit on how many refugees station can accept.

### Recruit to crew

If refugee gets along with your crew and generally has good time, why not to join you?

Available under following conditions:
1. friendly relationships with anyone from crew (overly talkative crewmates are suddenly useful)
2. average crew mood is at least 60
3. refugee is in good mood

If that option will be implemented, there should be a way to select who exactly you want to part with in other scenarios. And keeping some refugees should not damage faction relationships as long as they do not mind staying (mood conditions fulfilled but friendship may not be achieved yet).

### Eden drop off

After completion of Exodus quest line we have whole leviable planet. Anyone will happily go there, right?

Accepts everyone without limits. Minor credits reward.

### Ark ship

So you really do not want to see those guys anymore but other options do not work out. There is still out-of-airlock way but you heard it is bad for your karma or something. However you happened to have bunch of resources on hands. So lets build a ship, fill its stockpile with essentials, load up refugees and tell them to go on theirs own. You started like this, surely they will be just fine. If it works out — great, if not it is on them.

Following conditions need to be fill fulled to transfer ship:
* everyone on board is refugee of same faction
* no hostiles in sector for both you and refugees faction
* all areas should be reachable and safe without spacesuit, no impendent danger
* tools facility, at least 2 infra blocks
* enough oxygen generators and CO2 scrubbers to sustain crew with resources to run for at least 5 days
* food (agile dispenser is good enough), 5 days of resources
* somewhat reasonable bedroom, mess hall and toilet (as in per game room definitions), enough beds for everyone on board
* stocked medical cabinet, 2 units of medical supplies
* running hull stabilizer, enough steal to fully stabilize hull + 20% reserve
* not overloaded hyperdrive, navigation console, at least one refugee with navigation skill, enough fuel for 20 jumps
* operations console, at least one refugee with operations skill
* either at least one point defense turret per 500 points of ship size or 100 points of shield and shields console with skilled operator
* shuttle
* enough energy generation to support all of above, 5 days of resources

This option is not supposed to be resource efficient. If ship reaches destination you get normal reward for returning refugees + 50% refund on ship construction at destination point. If something happens to it along the way you get nothing.

Destination depends on faction:
* Civilians, merchants and minor factions — any friendly station in safe system, preferable of same faction.
* Military, androids, pirates and slavers — owned station.
* Cult on New Haven — one of owned fleets.

If there is no valid primary destination — random non-hostile station within reach. If that fails too — random sector and very high chance to disappear without trace.

What can happen for ship to not reach destination:
* If player is following it and present in sector — anything that can happen to NPC ship normally.
* During hyperline travel — random event with something like 15% chance. *For contentions engagement would be cool to toss recovery mission in this case if player fleet is close enough but that sounds like too much work.*
* Chance to get captured when passing hostile fleet with overwhelming combat characteristics. *Once again would be cool to make it event chain.*
* Not enough resources to reach destination, although there should be significant chance for refugees to deal with that on theirs own, especially in presence of friendly fleets.
